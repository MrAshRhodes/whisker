Every setting, explained →
More out of every button ↗
Remap up to 32 buttons, alone or with ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧. Press a button to record it. Your primary click stays protected unless you choose otherwise.
A move for every moment ↗
Clicks, shortcuts, media keys, Mission Control, switching Spaces and apps, scrolling, opening a file or website. Or doing nothing at all.
Different app. Different moves. ↗
Profiles for an app, a mouse, or one mouse in one app. The closest match lays its changes over Default; everything else is inherited.
A wheel that works your way ↗
One to twenty lines per notch, extra distance for quick spins, sideways scrolling with a key, and reversible directions for each mouse.
Your own sense of speed ↗
Per-mouse speed and acceleration, or a linear response, with named speed presets to switch between. Big jumps have a safety check; switching Whisker off restores macOS settings.
One mouse, or a few ↗
Mice and trackballs welcome. Trackpads and touch mice are left alone. Optional Input Monitoring tells multiple mice apart.
There when you need it ↗
Switch on or off, see your current profile and open settings from the menu bar. Start at login, or hide the icon altogether.
Settings you can take with you ↗
Plain JSON to export, import and back up. Whisker never saves over a file it couldn't read.
Updates on your terms ↗
Signed update support, with checks off by default. Downloaded copies update themselves when you ask, Homebrew installs update through Homebrew, and source builds are rebuilt by you.

At home
on your Mac.

Whisker has no accounts, analytics or telemetry.

Accessibility access lets Whisker change mouse input. It doesn't read what you type. Your settings stay on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Whisker/settings.json.

The only network request is an update check — off until you choose a schedule or press Check Now.

Your first-run guide →

Download

The latest release for macOS 14 or later comes as a DMG or a ZIP, each with a checksum file to check it against. It's signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple.

Download the latest release ↗

Or use Homebrew

brew install --cask mrashrhodes/tap/whisker

Downloaded copies can update themselves when you ask. Homebrew copies update through Homebrew.

Build from source instead

You'll need macOS 14+, Swift 5.9+, and Xcode or the free Command Line Tools: xcode-select --install.

git clone https://github.com/MrAshRhodes/whisker.git
cd whisker
make app

The app lands in dist/Whisker.app. Move it to Applications and open it, or use make run to build and open in one go.

Your build is signed just for your Mac, so Accessibility access may need resetting after a rebuild. Source builds can't update themselves: pull the latest source and build again.
